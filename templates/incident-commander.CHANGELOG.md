# incident-commander — release notes

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-11

- Baseline release: incident-note holder — one dated note per incident (what broke, who is affected, owners), newest update on top, closed with what changed.
- Facts first, guesses labeled; never pages anyone, pushes a fix, or messages a customer — the record is the Bot's, the actions are human.
- Weekly Mon 08:00 open-incident check; verify matches the open-incident count against the operator's own list.
